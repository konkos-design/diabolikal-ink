The house ofDiabolikal.

A small crew, a big green room and a dog who runs the place. Here's who you'll meet when you walk in.

Owner · Senior artist

Hardeep Sandhu

Hardeep built Diabolikal Ink and still does a big share of the work himself. He loves colour and comic-book style tattoos, but his portfolio runs from black & grey realism and big leg sleeves to fine line roses and script.

He's also the name that comes up most in our reviews: for cover-ups, for first tattoos, and for working late to finish a piece.

Be patient, be diligent, trust yourself, and never ever give up.Hardeep, on tattooing as a lifelong craft

Tattoo artist · Nautica Ink

Evgenia

Evgenia came back to the Fraser Valley after apprenticing and building her portfolio in the Okanagan. With a background in illustration, visual arts and architectural drafting, she has a serious eye for detail and a bit of a perfectionist streak.

She's booking appointments, with walk-ins on select days. Got an idea? Send her a DM.
